Fast and in your control

GPU or CPU. Your choice.

EchoMeBetter uses your graphics chip for speed, or the processor if you prefer. When you stop writing, the model sleeps and frees its memory, then wakes on your next rewrite.

  • Power saving or high performance GPU
  • Choose how much of the CPU it may use
  • Light and dark mode, like your system
Questions

Good to know

Is EchoMeBetter free?

Yes, for individuals. You can use it for your own writing, including for your job or studies, free of charge. Use by or for an organization, or any commercial use, needs a separate agreement under the Thelivu License.

Does my text ever leave my computer?

No. The model runs inside Chrome on your device. The extension only connects to Hugging Face to download the model and styles you choose. Your text is never sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.

Why is the first download so large?

It is a complete writing model, Thelivu, that runs on your own hardware instead of in the cloud. It downloads once, resumes if interrupted, and works offline afterwards.

What do I need to run it?

Chrome 116 or later on a desktop computer. A graphics chip makes rewrites fastest; without one, EchoMeBetter runs on the processor.

Which websites does it work on?

Any text box you can type in: email, documents, chat, issue trackers and forms. Right-click works everywhere with no extra permission; keyboard shortcuts and press and hold ask once for site access. Password fields are always left alone.
