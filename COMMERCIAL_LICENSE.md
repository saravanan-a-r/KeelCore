## When you need a commercial agreement

You need one if you want to do any of the following (see Sections 2.8, 5 and

- use EchoMeBetter for any commercial purpose, directly or indirectly;
- deploy it for an organization, or provide it to staff, members, or
  customers;
- include the model or code in a product, service, website, app, API, or
  automated system;
- rewrite or edit text for other people for payment using EchoMeBetter;
- use the model, or its outputs, outside the official extension, including
  hosting it or running it in your own software;
- carry out research for, on behalf of, or funded by a for-profit
  organization;
- use the model or its outputs to build, train, fine-tune, or evaluate other
  models; or
- buy or acquire the model, its adapters, the code, or the rights in them.

## What can be offered

Every agreement is negotiated case by case. Options include:

- **Commercial use license**: use of the extension or the model inside an
  organization or product, under agreed limits.
- **Organization license**: deployment of the official extension to your
  staff or members.
- **Research access**: access to the model outside the extension for a
  defined research project.
- **Purchase**: acquisition of the model, its adapters, the code, and the
  related intellectual property rights, in whole or in part.
